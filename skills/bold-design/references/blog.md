# Blog

A reading surface in the costume. The article is set on the canvas, in the same grotesque, at lede size. It is not a cream column with a serif.

## Opening

Top bar, same as the other recipes, with the publication's mark and word links. One paper action if there is a real one (subscribe, start). Otherwise the bar has no fake primary.

The title is display type, left, naked. No category kicker, no author line above it. Author, date, and length sit under the title as a mono label, Ash, 11px or larger.

The first viewport holds the title and the first paragraph. The Action, if the piece has one, sits after the first paragraph or in the bar, still visible at 390px.

## Body

- Measure under 65–75ch. Size 1.25rem, line-height 1.5, tracking -0.02em, color Lede or Paper on Canvas.
- More space above a heading than below it. Headings inside the article are title size, not a second display.
- Code, figures, and quotes sit in a square frame. Code has a mono filename bar. No rainbow highlighter required; identifiers may use Code.
- A series or related list is a ruled index: index number, title, one line. Not cards. Not thumbnails unless the piece is about the image.
- The close is a frame with the next action in Action, or the next piece as a headline. Not a newsletter card with an illustration.

## Voice

The costume does not add hype. Controls name the action. Errors name the problem and the recovery. Do not invent pull quotes, subscribers, or read times the source does not have.
