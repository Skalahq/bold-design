# SaaS

A product shell in the costume. The product is proven by a working control, not by a screenshot in a card.

## Opening

Same top bar as admin: mark, word links, one paper quick action, one search field.

The first viewport is a split. Left: the offer in display type, naked, then a lede under 44rem. Right, or directly under the title on a phone: the Action and, if needed, one Paper action, then the instrument that proves the offer. The instrument is a real control in a well, with callouts if the mechanism has parts. It is not a device mock or a gradient panel.

## Body

- Feature sections are framed instruments, one mechanism each. Giant outlined type may sit behind the control. The heading outside the frame is a naked headline.
- Pricing, if the product has real prices, is a ruled index: name, what it includes, the Action on the row the visitor should take. It is not three equal cards. Do not invent prices.
- Settings are a square card on a well or dot field: mono labels, square fields, a segmented choice, one switch. One Action for the commit.
- A signed-in shell keeps the top bar. It does not grow a left nav. Section links stay words in the bar, or a framed index on the page when the list is the work.

## Proof

Show the control doing the job with the product's own nouns. Label illustrative data. Do not borrow another product's customers, benchmarks, or claims.
